Scan Instructions & Specifications

Adhere to standard neuro-imaging orientation protocols to ensure accurate feature representation:

01

Supported Image Types

Standard 2D MRI slices in JPEG, JPG, or PNG formats. Both contrast-enhanced T1-weighted and T2-FLAIR axial views are supported.

02

Input Transformation

Scans are preprocessed to 224 × 224 resolution and converted to single-channel luminance grayscale prior to tensor ingestion.

03

Diagnostic Classes

Evaluated against four diagnostic classifications: Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary Tumor, and No Tumor.

Research Advisory

Model outputs are probabilistic predictions derived from validation datasets and should be interpreted strictly in academic and investigative contexts.

CNN ARCHITECTURE

DenseNet121

Densely connected convolutional networks connect all layers directly, promoting feature reuse, strengthening gradient flow, and minimizing parameter count.

HEALTHCARE ML

MONAI

Medical Open Network for AI provides domain-optimized architectures, medical imaging datasets, and robust transformations for healthcare diagnostics.

COMPUTE ENGINE

PyTorch

Industry-standard accelerated computational backend executing GPU-accelerated tensor operations or fast CPU fallback inference.
